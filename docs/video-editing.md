# 12. Video Editing & Motion Graphics

> Crafting stories on the timeline: editing, color, sound, visual effects, and motion design.

## 🧭 Roadmap

1. Editing fundamentals (cutting, pacing, story) → 2. One NLE: DaVinci Resolve (free) or Premiere → 3. Color correction & grading → 4. Sound editing → 5. After Effects (motion graphics) → 6. Short-form (Reels/TikTok) specialty → 7. Portfolio & clients

| Skill | Why it matters | Learn it (free) | Advanced |
