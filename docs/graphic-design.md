# 10. Graphic Design

> Visual communication: branding, typography, layouts, and illustrations that make ideas land.

## 🧭 Roadmap

1. Design principles (contrast, alignment, hierarchy) → 2. Typography → 3. Color theory → 4. Software (Figma free → Illustrator/Photoshop) → 5. Real briefs and portfolio → 6. Branding identity → 7. Arabic typography (special skill!)

## 🛠️ Core Skills

| Skill | Why it matters | Learn it (free) | Advanced |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|----------|
| Design principles | Everything is built on these | [GCFGlobal — graphic design basics (free)](https://edu.gcfglobal.org/en/beginning-graphic-design/) | — |
| Typography | 90% of design is type | [Butterick's Practical Typography (free book)](https://practicaltypography.com/) | [Font Squirrel (free fonts)](https://www.fontsquirrel.com/) |
| Color theory | Emotion and meaning in palettes | [Color theory — Adobe (free article)](https://www.adobe.com/learn/design/color-theory.html) • [Coolors palette generator](https://coolors.co/) | — |
| Layout & composition | Order and rhythm on the page | [Layout principles — Canva School](https://www.canva.com/learn/design-basics/) | — |
| Adobe Illustrator | The vector industry standard | [Adobe free tutorials (Illustrator)](https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/tutorials.html) | — |
| Adobe Photoshop | Editing & composing images | [Adobe Photoshop tutorials (free)](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/tutorials.html) | — |
| Figma (free & modern) | Free, collaborative, increasingly used for print/identity too | [Figma Learn (official, free)](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/categories/360002051613-Getting-started) | — |
| Logo & brand identity | The highest-paying design work | [Identity Design basics — The Futur (free)](https://www.youtube.com/@thefutur) | — |
| Arabic typography | Huge regional demand, few specialists | [Tarek Atrissi Design — Arabic type resources](https://www.atrissidesign.com/) | — |
| Illustration | Personal voice & premium style | [Procreate Handbook (official, free)](https://procreate.com/handbook/) | — |
