# 16. Writing & Content Creation

> Using words to inform, persuade, and build audiences: copywriting, storytelling, and publishing.

## 🧭 Roadmap

1. Writing fundamentals (grammar + clarity) → 2. Storytelling structure → 3. Pick a lane (copywriting / blogging / script-writing / technical writing) → 4. Publish consistently (blog, X, LinkedIn) → 5. Learn SEO writing → 6. Build a portfolio (even 10 pieces)

## 🛠️ Core Skills

| Skill | Why it matters | Learn it (free) | Advanced |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|----------|
| Clear writing (clarity first) | The base of everything | [Julian Shapiro — Writing Well handbook (free)](https://www.julian.com/guide/write/intro) • [Purdue OWL (free writing lab)](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/purdue_owl.html) | — |
| Grammar & punctuation | Credibility starts here | [Grammarly Handbook (free)](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/) • [Purdue OWL grammar](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/grammar/index.html) | — |
| Storytelling | Stories stick; facts slide off | [Pixar storytelling rules (free)](https://www.aerogrammestudio.com/2013/03/07/pixars-22-rules-of-storytelling/) | — |
| Copywriting (selling words) | Ads, emails, landing pages | [Copyblogger — free training](https://copyblogger.com/blog/) | — |
| Blogging & SEO writing | Compounding audience growth | [Ahrefs blogging for business (free course)](https://ahrefs.com/academy) | — |
| Content strategy | Consistency beats inspiration | [HubSpot content marketing (free cert)](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/content-marketing) | — |
| Technical writing | Documentation jobs pay very well | [Google Technical Writing (FREE course)](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing) | [Technical Writing Two (free)](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/two) |
| Scriptwriting (YouTube/podcast) | The biggest creator format now | [Screenwriting — BBC/Pixar analyses + The Script Lab (free)](https://thescriptlab.com/) | — |
| Social media writing (X/Twitter, LinkedIn) | Short-form hooks | [HubSpot — social media writing (free guide)](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/how-to-write-attention-grabbing-social-media-content) | — |
